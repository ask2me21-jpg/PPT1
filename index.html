Tell Me About Yourself — 4 Parts

06 / 17

⏱ Keep it 60–90 seconds. Make it simple and clear.

1

Who You Are

Name, city, degree

2

Family Background

Small family, parents' work

3

What You Bring

Projects, skills, learning

4

Why This Role

Why this job / company

💡 Always end with: "I would be happy to answer any questions."

✅

What to Say — Do's

07 / 17

✔ Start with your name and degree — keep it short.

✔ Mention one good project — what it does and what you learned.

✔ Talk about your learning mindset — "I like to learn new things."

✔ Connect to the role — "My Java skills match this job."

🎤

How to Sound

✔Confident but humble
✔Clear and slow — don't rush
✔Natural — don't memorize like a script
✔Smile and make eye contact

💡 Practice out loud at least 10 times. Record yourself to catch filler words.

❌

What to Avoid — Don'ts

08 / 17

✖ Don't ramble — 5 minutes about everything is bad.

✖ Don't rush — 15 seconds sounds nervous and unprepared.

✖ Don't read from a script — it sounds fake and robotic.

✖ Don't mention irrelevant details — 10th/12th marks, unrelated hobbies.

✖ Don't undersell or oversell — be honest and confident.

⚠️

Common Phrases to Avoid

✖"I'm not good at anything..."
✖"I just want any job..."
✖"My big brother / small sister..."
✖"Actually... um... basically..." (filler words)
✖Negative comments about past teachers or college

💡 Never share weaknesses or put yourself down — even as a joke.

Common Mistakes Freshers Make

11 / 17
📄

Mistake #1: Reciting Your Resume

The interviewer already has your resume. Don't repeat marks and grades — tell them something new and interesting.

🗣️

Mistake #2: Using Wrong Words

"Big brother" → elder brother · "Small sister" → younger sister · "Myself" → I am

⏳

Mistake #3: Rambling or Rushing

5 minutes of life story or 15 seconds of mumbling — both create a bad impression. Aim for 60–90 seconds.

😞

Mistake #4: Negative Self-Talk

Never say "I'm not good at anything." Replace with "I'm learning and growing."

💬

Complete Sample Introduction

12 / 17
Part 1 · Who You Are

Hello, my name is [Your Name]. I am from [City Name].

Part 2 · Family Background

I come from a small and happy family. My father is a [occupation] and my mother is a [homemaker / occupation]. I have one younger sister who is studying in [class / college].

Part 3 · What You Bring

I am a final-year Computer Science student at [College Name]. I built a task management web app using React and Node.js. More than 200 students in my college used it. This project taught me problem-solving and teamwork. I am a curious learner and I keep improving my skills through projects and online courses.

Part 4 · Why This Role

I am excited about this opportunity because my background in full-stack development matches this role. I want to contribute to a real product team and learn from experienced people.

I would be happy to answer any further questions. Thank you.

✔ Simple words ✔ 60–90 seconds ✔ Includes family ✔ Natural ending

Soft Skills Preparation Tips

15 / 17
✍️

Write & Practice

✔Write your introduction and practice aloud.
✔Record yourself — catch filler words.
✔Time it — aim for 60–90 seconds.
🤝

Mock Interviews

✔Practice with friends or seniors.
✔Get feedback on body language.
✔Ask about clarity and confidence.
👁️

Non-Verbal Cues

✔Maintain eye contact (not staring).
✔Smile naturally.
✔Sit upright, hands relaxed.
✔Avoid fidgeting or looking down.
📚

Research & Prepare

✔Research the company and role.
✔Prepare 2–3 questions to ask.
✔Know your resume inside-out.
